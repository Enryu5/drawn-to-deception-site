Frequently asked questions

Quick fixes for common issues.

How many players do I need?

A game needs 4 to 6 players, each on their own iPhone with an internet connection. Playing in the same room is the most fun, since you'll want to discuss who the traitor is.

How do I start a game?

One player taps Host, enters a nickname and gets a 4-character room code. Everyone else taps Join and enters that code. When at least 4 players are in the lobby, the host can start the game.

It says the room was not found or is full.

Check that the room code is typed exactly as shown on the host's screen. A room holds up to 6 players. If it's full, ask the host to start a new room.

I got disconnected in the middle of a game.

Open the app again right away. The game tries to reconnect you to your room automatically. If a player doesn't come back within a few seconds, the game may end for everyone (for example, if the traitor leaves). A stable Wi-Fi or mobile data connection gives the best experience.

My nickname or prompt isn't accepted.

If the text field shakes, the text contains a word that our filter blocks, or it's longer than the limit. Nicknames can be up to 12 characters, prompts up to 20. Try a different word.

I can't hear any sound.

Check that your iPhone isn't in Silent mode and that the volume is turned up.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

The short version
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We don't save your nickname, drawings, prompts or votes. They only exist in our game server's memory while your game room is open.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

This policy explains how Drawn to Deception ("the game", "we", "us"), made by Graphica Games, handles information when you play.

Information used while you play

To run a multiplayer game, your device sends the following to our game server, which passes it on to the other players in your room:

InformationWhy it's needed
Nickname and chosen avatarSo other players in your room can see who you are
Your drawingsSo everyone can see and vote on them
Prompts you typeTo pick what everyone draws
Your votesTo decide who gets voted out
Room code and a random session IDTo keep you in the right room and let you reconnect if your connection drops

You don't need to give us your real name, email address, phone number or location to play.

How long it's kept

This information is held only in the game server's memory while your room is open. It is never written to a disk or database. It is erased when the room closes or the server restarts, and we can't recover it afterwards.

Server logs

To keep the server running, it writes technical log messages such as room codes, random session IDs and connection events. These logs don't contain nicknames, drawings, prompts or IP addresses. They are kept for 7 days and are only used to fix problems.

Your connection

Like any online game, connecting to our server means your device's IP address is visible to the server so it can send game data back to you. We don't log or store IP addresses. The server is hosted by Amazon Web Services.

On your device

The game doesn't save any personal information on your iPhone. The nickname and prompt filter runs entirely on your device. Sound and vibration features don't send any data anywhere.

Other players

Your nickname, avatar and drawings are shown to the other players in your room. We recommend playing with people you know. Our filter blocks many offensive words, but it can't catch everything.

Apple

The game is distributed through Apple's App Store and TestFlight. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and we don't store the information used during play.

Your choices

Because we don't keep your game data after your room closes, there is nothing stored for us to show you or delete. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us and we'll respond as soon as we can.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
